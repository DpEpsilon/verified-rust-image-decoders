The shipped core (this directory, 1,557 verified obligations, spec 1.1) implements the strict behaviour. `policy/` holds the FROZEN BMP/DIB policy spec (`policy/POLICY-SPEC.md`, hashes in `policy/FREEZE.txt`) and `policy/THEOREMS-POLICY-DELTA.md` (READ BOTH FIRST: they define the policy record, the named profiles Strict / GdkCompat-2.42.12 / ImageCrateCompat-0.25.10 / RecommendedBeta, the derived header record handed from MH to MP/MR, the Strict bridge, and which theorems stay universal).
