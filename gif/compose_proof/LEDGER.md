## Proved result and scope

`compose_proof` proves THEOREMS.md's T5 (the interlace), T7 (the drawing), T8
(the disposals and `Saved`) and T4 (the colour lookup and the transparent rule),
plus T12's per-frame charges and the T13/T4 pixel clause, for Strict's
`lzw_tail` and `index_range = Reject` on frames whose `frame_ok` facts hold:

- `interlace_row` is `IR`, and `IR` is a bijection on `[0, h)` for
  `1 <= h <= MAX_DIMENSION`; the pass counts are `interlace_pass_sizes`; the
  inverse `back` is explicit and proved.
- Every drawn pixel of a frame inside the screen has its four bytes strictly
  inside the canvas, distinct stream positions give distinct cells, and the
  coordinate sums stay inside `2·MAX_DIMENSION`.
- `draw_index` equals `draw_step`; `draw_all` equals `draw_fold`/`draw_run`;
  `composite`/`next_frame` equals `MC` for the canvas, for `Saved` and for the
  first error, with no partial frame.
- Disposal 0/1/2/3 act on the clipped rectangle, the save and restore use the
  same offsets, and an empty clipped rectangle writes nothing.
- Under `Clip` a position is written iff its coordinates are inside the screen.
- Every alpha-255 pixel carries a table entry's RGB and every other pixel is
  `(0,0,0,0)`.
- The drawing loop runs `w·h` steps inside `C_CALL·(Δ + A)`; disposal and restore
  fit the previous frame's `w·h`; the `Saved` fill is the reserving call's `4·N`.

## Tools and runtime validation

`tests/compositor.rs` reads `tests/expected/compositor.json`, generated by
`tools/gen_compositor_cases.py` from the 30 M4 vector records through
`gif_oracle.py`, and replays 42 frames through the verified code:

- `compositor_matches_the_oracle`: every returned canvas equals the oracle's,
  frame by frame, including the `ColorIndexOutOfRange` frames.
- `draw_index_agrees_with_composite`: the per-index sink path and `composite`
  agree on the canvas and `Saved`.
- `interlace_row_matches_g11`: `h` in 1..300 plus the wire heights, against a
  direct transcription of G11, with the bijection checked.
- `rectangle_beyond_the_screen_writes_nothing`: the Q18 regression, for disposal
  2, the save, the restore and the drawing.

## Integration scope (M5)

The module moves into `gif_verus` with its own model; the parser proof supplies
`Frame` from `MB`'s frame header record and the active table's borrowed range,
the allocator proof supplies the `Canvas`/`Saved`/`LzwTable` reservations, and
the LZW core's loop calls `draw_index` once per emitted index. The interfaces
that meet are named in `README.md`.
