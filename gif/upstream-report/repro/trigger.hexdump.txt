trigger.gif -- 80 bytes. Reproduces the disposal-2 out-of-bounds memset in
gdk-pixbuf's animated-GIF iterator (io-gif-animation.c, GDK_PIXBUF_FRAME_DISPOSE).
Tiny hand-checked geometry: logical screen 4x2, second frame's left edge at x=6.

Why the crash: the graphic control extension at 0x29 sits before frame 1 and
gives that frame disposal method 2 ("restore to background"). When the iterator
renders frame 2 it first runs the disposal for frame 1, which clears
MIN(6 + 2, 4) - 6 = -2 pixels, i.e. (x_end - x_offset) * 4 = -8 bytes. Passing
that to memset yields size_t 18446744073709551608. Frame 2 is needed: the
disposal only runs when there is a frame after the bad one to render.
