# M3c proof report

**Stopping reason:** Finished the requested T7, T8, L4, L5 and L6 proofs. Verus 0.2026.11.02.8bf68ee, release profile, Rust toolchain 1.99.1-x86_64-unknown-linux-gnu. Final `./verify.sh` run 121 reports `verification results:: 142 verified, 0 errors` in `runs/121.log`. This dispatch made ten verifier runs, numbered 112–121. `cargo test` passed; `cargo test --test conformance -- --nocapture` reported `conformance: 111/111 vectors passed` and `test result: ok. 1 passed; 0 failed`.

- **T7 proved:** `parse` has the live postcondition `model::parses_to(bytes@, r)` for every byte slice, without a precondition. `parses_to` compares the exact first error or the count, order, hexadecimal string contents, payload endpoints, opaque flag and nominal side of every entry against the independently defined `mp`. The loop carries the accepted offsets and a prefix relation to the model walk. The executable checks and allocation pattern remain unchanged, apart from replacing the four-byte magic slice comparison with four equivalent byte comparisons; the conformance suite still passes.
- **T8 proved:** `lemma_t8_result` establishes the equivalence between model provenance and the actual result for every candidate entry list, comparing all four R1 record fields; `parse_with_t8` applies it to a concrete `parse(bytes)` result. Every decoder-after-parse wrapper derives `parsed_inv` from the same parse result through `lemma_parse_ok_gives_p` and `lemma_p_exec_inv`, without an assumed provenance fact. The decoder contracts and T1–T6 safety proofs remain in force.
- **L4 proved:** `lemma_l4_repeated_entries` covers every `0 ≤ k ≤ 4096`, including the empty base case; `lemma_l4_entry_count_exactly_4096` instantiates the 32,776-byte fixture.
- **L5 proved:** `lemma_l5_count_before_suffix` covers every suffix of length at most `67,108,864 − 32,784`; `lemma_l5_vectors` instantiates the empty suffix, `BAD`, and `none · BE32(7)`.
- **L6 proved:** `lemma_l6_invalid_before_count` covers every `u32 q < 8`, and `lemma_l6_oob_before_count` every `u32 q > 8`, with the declared size fixed at 32,784. `lemma_l6_vectors` instantiates `q=7`, `q=4,294,967,295`, and the valid `q=8` boundary.

**Earlier-attempt failure:** The prior draft compared a slice with the byte-string literal `b"icns"`, but that comparison did not establish equality with the independently defined `icns_magic()` sequence at the model call sites. Four direct byte comparisons provide the missing bridge. Its old loop also retained a quantified `entry_inv` alongside the offset-sequence relation; the final proof derives `parsed_inv` from `mp` success instead of carrying that redundant quantified invariant.

**Remaining beyond this dispatch:** L3 belongs to a later milestone. The 102 small model-vector comparisons, all 40 individual witness reports and the ten deliberate mutations in the manifest's full non-vacuity protocol were not performed here; this report does not claim they were. No additional allocation or trust assumption was introduced. The assurance boundary remains Verus/vstd contracts and faithful compilation, conditional on successful allocation and ordinary process stack availability.
