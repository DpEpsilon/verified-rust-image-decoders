Conclusion first, for PNG-Q7 (freeze of RecommendedBeta and the cap values). Numbers are files; "ran" means the pure-Python oracle produced Strict and RecommendedBeta outcomes (20,502 of 21,966 files; the rest are in Coverage below). **RecommendedBeta (draft 0.2) holds up on real files, and almost all of its effect on incidental assets comes from two of its 15 fields.**

**Caps (PNG-Q5).**
- Over the proposed caps there are 48 files (12 with a side above 16,384; 42 above 16.7 Mpx; 9 above 33.5 Mpx; 31 incidental). With the caps raised in the oracle, RecommendedBeta accepts 40 of them (all 31 incidental assets, 9 test); 5 of the 31 incidental files are rejected by Strict even with the caps raised (`TrailingBytes` 3, `ChunkOutOfOrder` 2) and accepted by RecommendedBeta. The other 8 were not accepted: five declare 1 to 2,300 Gpx and are rejected or crash every decoder (`image` crashes on the 935,820 x 854,554 file; the tiny 4,294,967,295 x 536,883,232 file errors everywhere); one declares a zero height; and two are valid images the incumbents decode but beyond the oracle's 134 Mpx ceiling, which it did not attempt (`gdk-pixbuf/tests/large.png`, 25,200 x 33,300 = 839 Mpx, 2.4 MiB; `opencv_extra` `read.png`, 13,043 x 13,917 = 181 Mpx, 0.7 MiB).
- Every incumbent decodes the 31 incidental over-cap files (`image`, libpng, gdk-pixbuf loader and `gdk_pixbuf_new_from_file`, glycin). libpng and gdk-pixbuf appear to fail on them in a first run only because the C harness refuses canvases above 16 Mpx; the numbers above come from runner copies with that limit raised to 2,000 Mpx (`incumbents-big` phase, see the docstring). The largest incidental file is 60.9 Mpx (244 MiB as RGBA8); the largest file that the incumbents decode is the 839 Mpx test file (`image`, both gdk-pixbuf paths and glycin decode it; the libpng runner, run under a 16 GiB address-space limit, crashed on it). Covering every incidental file as RGBA8 needs an output cap of at least 244 MiB (61 Mpx); a 256 MiB cap (67 Mpx) covers all of them and leaves the two test files above it (181 and 839 Mpx) out. For RGBA16 output (16-bit sources) the same pixel counts need twice the bytes; the largest 16-bit incidental file is 3840 x 1600 = 6.1 Mpx and the largest 16-bit file overall is 1000 x 10500 = 10.5 Mpx (test).
- The per-side cap of 16,384 rejects 5 valid files that every incumbent decodes: 65,535 x 1 and 1 x 65,535 (gdk-pixbuf), 524,288 x 2 and 2 x 524,288 (Ruffle), 32,768 x 100 (Firefox); all five are test files and all are under the area cap. No incidental asset has a side above 16,384.
- Thin images: 41 files with aspect at least 32:1 and a long side at least 1,024; Strict accepts 32 of the 40 that ran; of the other 8, `InvalidDimensions` 5 (the per-side cap, above), `CrcMismatch` 2, `InvalidChunkType` 1.
- No file in the corpus is a multi-monitor screenshot (see `png-STATS.md`); the over-cap evidence is wallpapers and a diagram, so it shows that large PNG files exist and that the incumbents and the oracle decode them, not what size screenshots reach. The owner's choice of `MAX_BYTES` is unconstrained by anything in this corpus above 244 MiB for RGBA8 and 488 MiB for RGBA16.

**Counts that reconcile with the earlier packet.** The 737 and 748 of PNG-Q7 are counts of synthetic inputs (see `png-STATS.md`); this pass does not use them.

## Method notes and caveats

- The Python oracle runs on every file up to 1 Mpx (20,500 files in total ran, including all files with a flag or in the 1-in-8 sample between 1 and 16.7 Mpx); 1,421 files between 1 Mpx and the cap were not run (no anomaly flag, not in the sample): their incumbent results exist, their oracle outcomes do not. Of those, none has a side above 16,384 or is thin (those were run in a second step, `--force-thin`).
- Streams of files without APNG chunks are taken from the still outcome (`decode_frames` returns the default image after the same walk), not run separately.
- Ablation shows which field turns a file from accepted into rejected when reverted alone. It does not show how often a field would matter on an arbitrary web or desktop population; two repositories supply 95 of the 144 `trailing` flips (`markpendlebury/ultrawide-wallpapers`) and 51 of the 62 incidental `ancillary_order` flips (`dharmx/walls`), so the incidental rates are driven by the tools those repositories used.
- GdkCompat and ImageCrateCompat were not run: the GIF pass did not run its compatibility profiles either, and PNG-Q14 defers both until Strict and RecommendedBeta are proved.
- The incumbents are libpng 1.6.48, gdk-pixbuf 2.42.12 (loader and `gdk_pixbuf_new_from_file`), png 0.18.1 through `image` 0.25.10 (still and frame loop), and glycin's loader code (png revision e917d6a). The Rust runners cap nothing; the C runners' 16 Mpx harness limit was raised for the large files as described.
- Fuzz-derived files are 342 of the files that ran (1.7%) and 255 of the fuzz class are one corpus, so fuzz-class rates say little about fuzzers in general.
