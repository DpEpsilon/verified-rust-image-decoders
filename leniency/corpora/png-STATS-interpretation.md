Conclusion for the owner questions, then the limits of the sample.

**Q5 (resource caps, 16,384 per side, 64 MiB RGBA8 output).** The corpus has 42 files whose IHDR claims more than 16.7 megapixels (the cap) and 12 with a side above 16,384; 9 claim more than 33.5 megapixels. Most are not usable images: of the 42, 10 are test inputs, one is fuzzer-derived, and the declared sizes of the largest are decompression bombs or deliberate limit tests (`image-png`'s 4,294,967,295 x 536,883,232 header, a 935,820 x 854,554 gdk-pixbuf crasher, sharp's 65,536 x 65,536 `uint32-limit.png`, OpenImageIO's 46,340 x 46,340 `bomb.png`). The rest are real: 31 incidental assets above 16.7 Mpx, 30 of them wallpapers (7680 x 4320 8K images, 6000 x 4000 photographs and similar, from `dharmx/walls` 18, `neonvoidx/pics` 6, `markpendlebury/ultrawide-wallpapers` 4, `k1ng440/Wallpapers` 2) and one a 6868 x 8872 diagram vendored in Firefox's tree (60.9 Mpx, RGBA8, 4.0 MiB, the largest real image by area); the next is a 10000 x 5625 wallpaper (56.2 Mpx, 1-bit palette, 1.2 MiB). Documentation and website screenshots stay under the cap: the largest in `github/docs`, `kubernetes/website`, `godotengine/godot-docs`, `mdn/content` and `gnome-user-docs` is 16.8 Mpx (4096 x 4096), and the largest side among them is 5,172. **No file in the corpus is a multi-monitor screenshot**: the wide images (168 with width at least 3,840 and aspect at least 2.2:1) are ultrawide and 8K wallpapers and artwork, and 67 files are at least 5,120 wide with aspect 2.5 to 8 (65 incidental). So the corpus shows that real PNG files exist above the proposed output cap (31 incidental files, 0.4% of the 8,296 incidental assets), but it says nothing about screenshots of three 4K monitors (about 25 Mpx at 3 x 3840x2160 side by side, 33 Mpx at 3 x 5120x2160). The outcome of each over-cap file under Strict (cap rejection) and with the caps raised is in `png-RECOMMENDEDBETA-PASS.md`.

**Q5 (the 5 MiB question the GIF corpus could not ask).** With a per-file cap of 64 MiB, 240 files lie between 5 and 64 MiB (232 incidental, 7 test, 1 fuzz; 2,381 MiB, 52% of the corpus bytes); none exceeds 64 MiB (largest 44.2 MiB, a 4537 x 2552 RGBA wallpaper). A 5 MiB cap, as in the GIF corpus, would have removed all of them, 232 of them incidental assets (wallpapers and a few large documentation images). The input cap of 256 MiB is not approached.

**Long thin images.** 128 files have aspect at least 32:1 and 41 of those also have a side of at least 1,024 (25 incidental, 13 test, 3 fuzz): 1 x 65,535 and 65,535 x 1 from gdk-pixbuf, 2 x 524,288 and 524,288 x 2 from Ruffle (these two exceed the per-side cap), and sprite strips and banners among the incidental assets. 276 files are one pixel wide or high.

**Colour types, bit depths and interlacing.** RGBA8 36%, RGB8 30%, indexed 8-bit 15%, indexed 4-bit 7.4%; 16-bit images are 385 files (1.8%: 0.4% of incidental assets, 2.1% of test files, 19.9% of fuzz files); Adam7 interlaced files are 359 (1.6%; 0.3% of incidental assets). The corpus therefore tests the 16-bit entry point and Adam7 mostly through test suites. APNG: 123 files (4 incidental, 112 test, 7 fuzz); 6 have an `acTL` frame count different from the number of `fcTL` chunks, 9 a non-contiguous sequence number, 1 has frame chunks without `acTL`; none has a frame rectangle outside the canvas or an invalid dispose or blend operation (those cases are covered by hand-written vectors, not by the corpus).

**Damage visible at the chunk level** (no decoder involved; `png-RECOMMENDEDBETA-PASS.md` has the Strict and RecommendedBeta outcomes). 98.1% of files are clean by the scan's definition (98.6% of incidental assets, 98.4% of test files, 75.8% of fuzz files). Among the 8,289 incidental assets with an IHDR: 107 have bytes after IEND (96 in `markpendlebury/ultrawide-wallpapers`, 4 in `neonvoidx/pics`, 4 in Firefox's tree, 3 elsewhere; median 427 KiB, minimum 1 byte, maximum 12.9 MB, so mostly large appended payloads, not padding), none lacks IEND, none has a bad CRC, none has surplus data after the end of the zlib stream, none has a zlib error. Ancillary chunks after IDAT (tEXt, tIME, eXIf, pHYs and similar, legal in PNG) occur in 238 incidental assets (`gimp-help` 64, `dharmx/walls` 63, `kubernetes/website` 26). Among test files: 126 lack IEND (104 of them are the truncated variants in the `png/progressive` directories of web-platform-tests and its copies in Servo and Chromium), 41 have trailing bytes, 17 have a bad CRC, 17 a zlib error, 3 surplus compressed bytes inside IDAT, 1 surplus decompressed output. Fuzz-derived files carry most of the files without IDAT (19 of 23) and of the duplicated single-instance chunks (15 of 18).

**What the corpus cannot show.**
- Sampling bias. Every file comes from a decoder or browser test suite, a documentation tree, a theme, a wallpaper or dotfiles repository, not from a crawl, a camera or a screenshot tool. Wallpaper repositories (1,123 files, 14% of the incidental class and 30 of its 31 files above 16.7 Mpx) are curated images, mostly 8-bit RGB or RGBA from graphics tools; documentation screenshots come from a small number of operating systems and tools. Each file is counted once even where Chromium, WebKit and Firefox ship the same bytes (5,553 exact duplicates were dropped, so popularity is not weighted), but near-duplicates (the same image re-encoded) are not detected.
- Fuzz share. 347 files (1.6%) are fuzzer-derived by path; 255 of them are one corpus (`strongcourage/fuzzing-corpus`). The fuzz class is a path heuristic: files from fuzzers that are stored under test names are counted as test files, so the 98.4% clean rate among test files measures regression-test inputs, not ordinary files.
- Test-suite bias. 61% of files (13,323) are test files, and 2,985 of them are Serenity's `Tests/LibGfx` and `Base/res` assets, 1,492 Qt's, 1,276 and 1,242 Chromium's and WebKit's (capped samples of 36,877 and 20,622 paths, taken in a fixed pseudo-random order), so the anomaly counts above reflect what those projects chose to keep.
- Caps. Per-repo caps (file and MiB) sampled the large trees: Chromium, WebKit, Firefox, Servo, wpt, `gimp-help`, `github/docs`, `mdn/content` and the wallpaper repositories stopped at their cap (`stopped_by` in the table of `png-STATS.md`); 112,149 PNG paths were in the trees read and 21,966 distinct files are in the corpus. The hold-out (`holdout-png.tsv`, 10 whole repos, 5,059 files) was chosen by name before any statistic was computed.
- Unreachable or empty sources. Git LFS storage is not reachable from this sandbox: 10,334 files are LFS pointers and were dropped (`SixLabors/ImageSharp` 1,515, `microsoft/vscode-docs` 5,149, `blender/blender` 3,656, `KhronosGroup/KTX-Software` 14), so the corpus has no VS Code or Blender documentation screenshots and no ImageSharp test images. Trees that could not be read: `blender/blender-manual`, `inkscape/inkscape-docs` and `MozillaSecurity/fuzzdata` (the clone failed; probably not hosted at those GitHub paths). Not reachable at all: Wikimedia Commons, `www.schaik.com` (PngSuite's home; the suite is present through libpng, image-png and stb copies), `philip.html5.org` (Philip Taylor's APNG suite; present through its WPT port), chromium.googlesource.com, wpt.live, gitlab.freedesktop.org. KDE's `breeze-icons` and `papirus-icon-theme` contribute almost nothing (their icons are SVG).
- Wallpapers are not screenshots. The owner's use case (very large multi-monitor screenshots) has no representative here; a screenshot differs from a wallpaper by its content (flat regions, text, sharp edges, many repeated rows, palette-friendly), and so by filter-type mix and compression ratio, which matter for decoder work and memory but not for the structural cases measured here.
- Licences. All files are NOASSERTION in the manifest; the repos license their code, not their images, and wallpaper repositories contain third-party art. Bytes are for local measurement only and stay in `~/corpora/png/`.
- "Incidental" is a path heuristic (repo kind plus the absence of a test-like word in the path); the first test token wins, so a documentation image under a `testing/` directory counts as a test file.
