2. **Totality — no finding.** `model_totality(b: &[u8])` has no `requires`. Its postconditions reject both a top-level `OutsideDomain` and a `Done` value ending in `OutsideDomain`. They also require, after every successful structural walk, a defined default-data result and a defined result for **every** in-range frame, not merely every returned frame (`src/frame_model.rs:33–46,169–187`). The walk contract distinguishes a genuine format error from success and excludes OOM (`src/callers.rs:33–44`). The certificate measures IDAT with one 32 KiB stack window; it then advances through every APNG job even if the default data or an earlier frame has a format error. A fresh reader goes through the measuring contract; a reused reader inherits the already-defined default result; an ordinary PNG uses the single-frame rule (`src/frame_model.rs:152–166,201–244`; `src/frame_state.rs:202–213`). The loop proves `is_some()`, not `is_ok()`. Keeping `End::OutsideDomain` in the projection therefore neither hides an unmeasured future frame nor obliges the shipping constructor to prepass future frames.
