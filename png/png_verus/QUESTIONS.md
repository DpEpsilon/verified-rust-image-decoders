# png_verus: open questions

Items where the frozen spec, the draft policy texts and `inflate_verus` leave a choice, or where the implementation found something the texts did not anticipate. In each case M1b follows the most literal reading, named below; nothing in `spec-frozen/`, `THEOREMS.md`, `ERRATA.md` or `REVIEW-*.md` was edited.

The council decided Q1, Q2, Q4 and Q5 on 2026-11-20 (report M1BQ, all unanimous; `../policy-draft/DECISIONS.md` "Council M1b-Q rulings"). Each section ends with its resolution. Q3 is unchanged (P2).

## Q1. `zlib_tail = IgnoreTail` with fewer than `R` bytes: `TrailingZlibData` or `TruncatedImageData`?

`inflate_verus` ignores bytes after a matching Adler-32 under `IgnoreTail` only when the output is exactly full (`finish` in `inflate_verus/src/inflate.rs`; README "`IgnoreTail` ignores bytes after a matching Adler-32 only when `produced == limit`", following THEOREMS.md and OPTIONS.md's "acts only after the Adler-32 matched and the output is exactly `R` bytes"). With fewer than `R` bytes it returns `Err(TrailingZlibData)`. `policy_oracle.py` `zlib_decompress` instead ignores the tail whenever `zlib_tail != 'Reject'`, and its caller then reports `TruncatedImageData` because `len(raw) < R`.

The two error names differ for one input class: a RecommendedBeta image stream that is short and has bytes after a matching Adler-32. M1b maps `Err(TrailingZlibData)` to `TruncatedImageData` when the profile is IgnoreTail and `produced < R` (`image.rs` `image_status`). The mapping is exact: under IgnoreTail inflate reports `TrailingZlibData` only after the Adler-32 matched, which is the case in which the oracle reaches its length check. Example (scratch only): a 2 × 2 greyscale image whose IDAT holds a zlib stream of one scanline followed by two bytes: Strict `TrailingZlibData`, RecommendedBeta `TruncatedImageData` in both the oracle and png_verus; without the mapping png_verus would report `TrailingZlibData` under RecommendedBeta. The rule needs a line in RecommendedBeta's model at P1 (Stage 2), or a decision that the inflate-level name should surface instead; no vector of the 810 pins it.

**Resolved (council M1b-Q1, (b)).** The inflate-level name surfaces: under `IgnoreTail` the tail is ignored only when the output is exactly `R` bytes, so the short stream with a tail is `TrailingZlibData`, as under Strict, and a short stream without a tail is `TruncatedImageData`. Remove `image_status`'s short-output `TrailingZlibData` to `TruncatedImageData` mapping (done, `src/image.rs`) and change `policy_oracle.py` to apply `IgnoreTail` only when `len(out) == limit` for image and frame streams (done, phase 2). Keep `inflate_verus`'s model, executable and proofs unchanged. Apply the rule to every Strict/RecommendedBeta-valued option record with `IgnoreTail`. Add still and fdAT-frame policy vectors for short output with and without a tail, short output followed by another complete stream, exactly-full output with a tail, and a wrong Adler-32 with a tail (done, phase 2: `policy/zlib-tail/short-*`, `full-with-tail` and `fdat-*` in `VECTORS-POLICY.json`; the same cases are in `tests/m1bq_rulings.rs`). PNG-Q1 is recorded as narrowed, not restated; ICC tail handling is left to D14.

## Q2. RecommendedBeta: a fcTL dropped for its CRC need not make the animation fail, and the fdAT after it is then inflated

OPTIONS.md (`crc_ancillary`) says "acTL and fcTL stay ancillary, so a bad-CRC one is dropped and the animation then fails structurally (R4)". That holds when the dropped fcTL's sequence number was the expected one. When the fdAT after the dropped fcTL carries the sequence number the walk expects next, the walk accepts the file, and the fdAT payload is appended to the preceding fdAT-based frame. Example (built in scratch only): `acTL(1 frame)`, IDAT, `fcTL(seq 0)`, `fdAT(seq 1)` holding the first half of a zlib stream, `fcTL(seq 2)` with a bad CRC, `fdAT(seq 2)` holding the second half, IEND. `policy_oracle.decode_frames(RecommendedBeta)` returns one frame decoded from both halves with `recovered = false`; Strict returns `CrcMismatch`. png_verus matches the oracle (its fdAT reader ends a frame only at a fcTL whose CRC matches, `reader.rs`).

This bears on the council's L-WALK-FIRST obligation ("never inflates the fdAT data of a frame whose fcTL failed its checks"). Under the literal reading of the oracle, the data after a CRC-dropped fcTL belongs to the previous frame, not to a frame of its own, so no rejected frame's stream is inflated; but a reader of L-WALK-FIRST could count the dropped fcTL as "a frame whose fcTL failed its checks". Also, no `recovered` event fires although the frame's data changed composition. Decision wanted at P1: keep (and state it in OPTIONS.md and L-WALK-FIRST), or reject a bad-CRC fcTL like fdAT (`crc_ancillary = SkipExceptFdat` extended to fcTL), which would make every such input `CrcMismatch` or an R4 fallback.

**Resolved (council M1b-Q2, (c)).** Neither: in the frames constructor's walk with APNG on, a bad-CRC fcTL that `crc_ancillary = SkipExceptFdat` would drop is an APNG structural error named `CrcMismatch` (the coordinator's choice on the council's 2–1–1 split over the name), raised at that chunk before its sequence number is read; under RecommendedBeta (`apng_errors = Static`) the frames entry returns the default image with R4, while still decoding and the fallback's re-walk drop the fcTL. Both examples above (the splice and the two-complete-streams case) now give frames: default image, `recovered = true`; still: default image, `recovered = false`. Recovery is selected by the walk site, not by the name; `crc_ancillary = Reject` makes the same fcTL the mandatory `CrcMismatch`, which `Static` never catches; a later bad fdAT CRC or malformed framing still surfaces from the fallback's re-walk. Done in `src/walk.rs`; `FdatReader` and `next_fctl` no longer recompute fcTL CRCs (`src/reader.rs`); tests in `tests/m1bq_rulings.rs`. The policy oracle and the new records followed in phase 2 (`policy/apng-static/fctl-bad-crc-*`). Update the policy oracle, constructor walk and frame reader consistently; existing bad-fcTL fallback expectations remain unchanged. L-WALK-FIRST covers the case only with the council's clarification (`../PLAN.md` M3): a fcTL whose CRC does not verify is a fcTL that failed its checks.

## Q3. The two `ImageCrateCompat-0.25.10` records of VECTORS-POLICY.json

`policy/crc-ancillary/fdat-frames/image-crate-compat` and `.../fdat-still/image-crate-compat` need `crc_ancillary = Skip`, `apng_errors = Prefix` and `after_idat = ImageRs`, which belong to the compatibility profiles (P2, out of scope for M1b). `tests/conformance.rs` counts them as skipped, never as passed: 808 of 810 records pass, 2 are skipped. ## Q4. API items of BRIEF.md without a model

`chunks(b, p)` with `ChunkStatus` and `try_icc_profile_with_policy` (PNG-Q4, Q13) are not implemented: SPEC P8.3 and P17 keep them outside the Stage-1 model, and PLAN.md P1 says they "need models first (D14–D16)". `Info` already carries the three-valued `Meta` (`Absent | Discarded | Present`) per item; under Strict nothing is ever `Discarded`.

**Resolved (council M1b-Q4, (a)).** Deferred to P1 under D14 and D15; nothing is dropped from the briefed API, and neither entry gets a stub or `unimplemented!`. M1b is complete without them (`LEDGER.md` "Not done in M1b").

## Q5. Representation choices (no spec text decides them)

- `Still16::rgba16` is `8·W·H` bytes with each 16-bit sample big-endian, because the guard reserves bytes (`try_zeroed`) and P20's `Output` site is byte-counted. A `Vec<u16>` would need a second element type in the guard. **Resolved (council M1b-Q5a, (a)):** kept, with the byte-to-sample relation recorded (`../BRIEF.md`; T13 notation: ERRATA E3).
- `Frames::try_new(b, p)` takes the profile, as BRIEF.md's API block writes it; there is no separate Strict-only constructor. `Frames` is not an `Iterator` (each view borrows the canvas), like GIF's. **Resolved (council M1b-Q5b, (b)):** renamed: `Frames::try_new(b)` is Strict and `Frames::try_new_with_policy(b, p)` takes a named profile; "not an `Iterator`" stands. Done in `src/png.rs`, the tests, `examples/png_probe.rs` and the leniency `verified` runner; constructor notation: ERRATA E4.
- `Info` byte ranges (iCCP name, eXIf) are `(offset, length)` pairs into the caller's input, so `Still` carries no lifetime. **Resolved (council M1b-Q5c, (a)):** kept, with the new M3 sign-off gate L-INFO-RANGE (`LEDGER.md`, `../PLAN.md`); checked accessors are optional and not required now.
- The RecommendedBeta RGBA16 entry (`try_decode_still16_with_policy`, PNG-Q13 (i)) has no oracle; M1b draws a substituted palette index (R3) as `(0, 0, 0, 65535)` and otherwise applies P13.3 with RecommendedBeta's walk and inflate options. **Resolved (council M1b-Q5d, (a)):** the entry is retained and its rule adopted as D16's model text (`../policy-draft/DECISIONS.md`); it remains unmodelled and outside every supported-policy claim until P1's independent evidence exists (`LEDGER.md`).

## Q6. Does T6's “table equals Crc on single bytes” mean the unfinalized polynomial remainder?

T6 describes both a 256-entry table equal to `Crc` on single bytes and a table-driven update equal to the preset-and-complemented message CRC. Those are different functions: the production table has `TABLE[0] = 0`, whereas the specified ISO CRC of the one-byte message `[0]` is `0xD202_EF8D` (both the bitwise model and the actual `crc32` call are checked in `witness_m3::crc_known_answers`, run 24).

Does the council intend the table clause to say `TABLE[i] = crc_bits(i, 8)` with neither preset nor complement, while the whole-message clause retains both? The M3 checkpoint proves exactly those two equations in `crc::make_table`, the static `TABLE` initializer, `crc::lemma_crc_update` and `crc::crc32`, against `model.rs`'s independent bitwise definition. It does not claim the literal, false equation `TABLE[i] = crc([i])`. No manifest or frozen-spec wording was changed; T6 remains partial regardless of this question because the ordering walk is not proved.
