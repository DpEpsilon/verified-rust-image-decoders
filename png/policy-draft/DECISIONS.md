# PNG owner decisions: changelog

The owner decided `QUESTIONS.md` PNG-Q1–Q15 on 2026-11-16 (Q16 needs no action yet); this file records how each decision was applied to the drafts, which became PNG-CORE-SPEC draft 0.2 and policy draft 0.2. Nothing is frozen and no freeze file exists. Results after applying them: 793 vectors (592 plus three OVER corner cases plus 198 profile vectors) agree with `png_oracle.py`, 62 of 62 errors exercised, inflate agrees with zlib on 750 vector streams, 200 random streams and 200 corruptions; Strict bridge 767 of 767 inputs, 0 differences (737 before the new vectors); obligation R over 370 Strict-accepted still inputs (364 before) and every Strict frame, 0 violations; RecommendedBeta accepts 224 of the 289 inputs that Strict rejects and an incumbent accepts (231 before); compatibility agreement of GdkCompat and ImageCrateCompat unchanged on every set. (Those are the 2026-11-16 numbers. The 2026-11-17 cap decision below changes them: 796 vectors, Strict bridge 773 of 773, obligation R over 372 inputs, RecommendedBeta 224 of 287.)

## Decisions and their effect

- **Q1, RecommendedBeta's field set.** The council's version: Strict plus thirteen full relaxations and `zlib_tail` narrowed to the new value `IgnoreTail` (compressed bytes after an Adler-32-checked end of the stream ignored; surplus decompressed output stays `ExcessImageData`, so the inflate limit stays `R`). A value change, not a field split: the record keeps 30 fields and inflate keeps five policy fields (Q12). The draft's fourteen relaxations were fourteen fields, so the council's version is 13 full ones plus the narrowed `zlib_tail`; Q11 then adds `apng_errors=Static`, so RecommendedBeta differs from Strict in 15 fields: fourteen full relaxations and the narrowed one. `surplus_output=Ignore` for compatibility profiles is open until a bounded rule and an R2 detector exist. R2 is no longer reachable in RecommendedBeta (reachable events: R1, R3, R4). `missing_iend=AtEof` is recorded as a design choice: `image` rejects a missing IEND. Recomputed: 224 of 289 (was 231); the seven inputs lost are all `ExcessImageData` (`vec:image/excess-data`, `vec:image/excess-row`, `probe:interlace-1x1-empty-pass-bytes`, `probe:excess-data-byte`, `probe:excess-data-row`, `wit:21-zlib-one-byte-long`, `wit:22-zlib-extra-row`); RecommendedBeta against `image` 686 to 679 of 721 and against the GDK loader 608 to 601 (fitted set), 326 to 320 and 297 to 291 (held-out A), 338/395 and 296/395 (held-out B, unchanged). GdkCompat and ImageCrateCompat keep draft 0.1's values (`zlib_tail=StopAtRaster` and `Ignore`). Files: `policy_oracle.py`, `OPTIONS.md`, `QUESTIONS.md`, `../BRIEF.md`, `../PLAN.md` (P1).
- **Q2 (a), CRC.** Strict checks every CRC. RecommendedBeta drops a bad-CRC ancillary chunk only after its length and bounds are checked (`chunk_at` runs first), R1 for tRNS, and rejects a bad CRC on IHDR, PLTE, IDAT and fdAT. The oracle previously treated fdAT as ancillary by its type case and would have skipped it, so the rule is the new value `crc_ancillary=SkipExceptFdat` (RecommendedBeta only; png 0.18.1 skips a bad fdAT, so ImageCrateCompat keeps `Skip`). It applies in `decode_still` too, where fdAT is otherwise ignored, and in the Q11 fallback walk. acTL and fcTL stay ancillary: a bad-CRC one is dropped and the animation then fails structurally (R4). `iend=Lenient` exempts IEND. Files: `policy_oracle.py`, `OPTIONS.md`.
- **Q3 (a).** Rounding `(v + 128) / 257` everywhere except GdkCompat (`HighByte`); exact samples through the 16-bit entry point. Text only (`OPTIONS.md` D2, `QUESTIONS.md`).
- **Q4 (a).** Raw samples; raw colour-chunk values and the ICC profile through a separate call; a malformed ICC payload never blocks pixels (only `try_icc_profile` inflates it; Strict's walk checks only the name and method byte); metadata access is `Absent | Discarded | Present`, plus `Failed(error)` for the ICC profile. Files: `OPTIONS.md` (`metadata_status`), `SPEC.md` (P8.3, P17), `../BRIEF.md` (API).
- **Q5, caps.** The proposed numbers are the provisional implementation baseline, held out of the freeze until the corpus pass (Q7), named constants in one section (P1), theorems symbolic. The corpus pass must test the 16,384 per-side cap and the 64 MiB output cap against large screenshots and long thin images. **The shared guard `MAX_BYTES` (67,108,864, frozen in `../../alloc-delta`, byte-identical in every core) is not raised until it is known what to raise it to; the owner chooses the value after the PNG corpus pass (very large multi-monitor screenshots; bounds to be raised eventually).** Recorded in `QUESTIONS.md` Q5, `../BRIEF.md`, `../PLAN.md`, `SPEC.md` P1 and `../../RELEASE-GATES.md` item 1. Adam7 defect fixed: `R ≤ H·(1 + 8W)` was wrong for interlaced images (more scanlines than `H`). Exact bound `R ≤ 8·W·H + S(H)` with `S(H)` the scanline count, `S(H) ≤ ⌈15H/8⌉ + 2 ≤ 2H + 2` (hand bound `8·S ≤ 15H + 29`, checked for `H ≤ 70,000`), so `R ≤ 2·MAX_OUTPUT + 2·MAX_DIMENSION + 2` (the earlier `+ MAX_DIMENSION` was also wrong: RGBA16 interlaced 1024 × 16,384 has `R = 134,248,448` against 134,234,112). Worked check: RGBA16 interlaced 1024 × 1024, `S = 1,920`, `R = 8,390,528` against the old 8,389,632. Peak heap per entry point is in `SPEC.md` P20 and `../BRIEF.md`. New check `spec-draft/check_bounds.py`. Files: `SPEC.md` (P1, P11, P20), `DESIGN-NOTES.md` (N4), `../BRIEF.md`, `../PLAN.md`, `../../RELEASE-GATES.md`.

- **Q5, caps: decided 2026-11-17 (supersedes the numbers above; the entry above is kept as history).** The owner raised the caps. (1) Per-side cap: 16,384 becomes the PNG wire limit 2^31 − 1; the output cap bounds the area. Five valid corpus files that every incumbent decodes (65,535 × 1, 1 × 65,535, 524,288 × 2, 2 × 524,288, 32,768 × 100) were rejected at 16,384 (`../../leniency/corpora/png-RECOMMENDEDBETA-PASS.md`). (2) RGBA8 output cap: 64 MiB (16.7 megapixels) becomes 256 MiB (67,108,864 pixels, 8192 × 8192 equivalent); the largest real image in the corpus is 60.9 megapixels (244 MiB) and 256 MiB covers all 8,296 incidental files. (3) The shared guard `MAX_BYTES` (per reservation, 64 MiB, frozen in `../../alloc-delta`) is being raised to 2^30 (1 GiB) by a separate change (alloc-delta erratum E16); PNG's caps now depend on it. A 67-megapixel RGBA16 output is 512 MiB and `Raw` (filtered data) is about 512 MiB (at most 640 MiB with the Adam7 scanline term), each a single reservation at most 2^30. That ends the open item above ("`MAX_BYTES` against the PNG sites", and the P20 text that said the effective caps were lower because of the guard): it was open, and is resolved by this decision, provided E16 lands. Input cap stays 256 MiB (superseded the same day: 2^30, see "Input-cap update" below); frames (65,536), total frame pixels (2^30) and ICC (16 MiB) are unchanged. Input-cap open edge: a stored (uncompressed) 16-bit RGBA PNG of 2^26 pixels is about 537 MB (an 8-bit RGBA one at the cap about 28 KB over 256 MiB) and is rejected `FileTooLarge`; raising `MAX_INPUT` later is a strict widening. GIF erratum E3 lesson: bit counts in `u32` overflow for inputs above 512 MiB, so the PNG and inflate proofs use 64-bit bit positions from the start.

## Open for the owner

- **`MAX_BYTES` against the PNG sites.** One reservation is limited to 67,108,864 bytes by the guard. `Raw` for a 16-bit RGBA image (about `8N` bytes) and the RGBA16 `Output` exceed it at the proposed caps (at `N = 2^24`, 128 MiB each), and 8-bit RGBA `Raw` (`4N + S(H)`) exceeds it at the output cap itself. So under the unchanged guard the effective caps are smaller than the table in P1 (for example 16-bit RGBA inputs above about 8.4 megapixels cannot reserve `Raw`). How P1 expresses that (extra caps on `R` and `8N`, or a lower `MAX_OUTPUT` until the guard is raised) is not decided here; the corpus pass sizes it.
- **`surplus_output=Ignore`** for compatibility profiles: needs a bounded rule and an R2 detector.
- **fdAT after a structural error.** In the Q11 fallback the walk keeps rejecting a bad-CRC fdAT but otherwise does not look at APNG chunk contents; whether a bad-CRC fcTL or acTL (dropped, then a structural error, then R4) should instead be rejected like fdAT is not decided.
- **Vectors for Q2 and Q11.** Added 2026-11-16: 198 profile-tagged vectors (793 in total) cover the fdAT CRC rule, the R4 fallback and its non-fallback cases, and every differing RecommendedBeta field (`spec-draft/SPEC.md` P19 describes the `profile` key). All 793 agree with the oracle.

## Guard-limit update — 2026-11-17

The owner approved alloc-delta erratum E16: `MAX_BYTES` is now 1,073,741,824
bytes (1 GiB), not 67,108,864 bytes (64 MiB). This supersedes the earlier plan
to choose the shared guard limit after the PNG corpus pass. DIB, TGA, ICNS,
ICO and GIF keep their existing 64 MiB output caps; a core may raise its cap
separately through its own erratum. PNG's owner-decided caps are now the PNG wire-limit dimensions and 256 MiB
RGBA8 output; freezing those values remains corpus-gated. The 1 GiB per-reservation guard accommodates the
512 MiB RGBA16 output for an 8192 × 8192 image and its filtered raw buffer;
the earlier statements that those proposed PNG reservations exceed the guard
limit describe the pre-E16 guard, not the current limit. Verification and
audit evidence, including pending DIB verification, is in
`alloc-delta/ERRATA.md` E16 and `RELEASE-GATES.md` item 1.

## Input-cap update — 2026-11-17

**Decided 2026-11-17 by coordinator pending owner objection.** `MAX_INPUT` is raised
from 268,435,456 bytes (256 MiB) to 1,073,741,824 bytes (2^30, 1 GiB). Reason: a
stored (uncompressed) 8192 × 8192 RGBA8 PNG is about 268 MB and exceeded 256 MiB,
so an image inside the output cap could be rejected `FileTooLarge` (the "open edge"
of the Q5 entry of 2026-11-17). The input is borrowed, not allocated, so no
reservation or peak-heap figure changes; inflate and walk positions are 64-bit
(`8·MAX_INPUT` = 2^33 does not fit `u32`, GIF erratum E3). At 2^30 every still
image within the output caps fits a stored encoding whose IDAT chunks average at
least 21 payload bytes; a stored APNG whose frame data together exceed the cap is
still `FileTooLarge` (frame data is bounded by `MAX_TOTAL_PIXELS`). The value stays
held out of the freeze with the other caps (Q7). If the owner objects, revert the
two constants and the text below; nothing else depends on the value.
