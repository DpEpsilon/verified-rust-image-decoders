# PNG policy option catalogue (draft 0.2, not frozen)

Draft 0.2 applies the owner's decisions of 2026-11-16 (`DECISIONS.md`). Changes to this file: `crc_ancillary` gains `SkipExceptFdat` (PNG-Q2), `zlib_tail` gains `IgnoreTail` (PNG-Q1), RecommendedBeta takes `apng_errors=Static` (PNG-Q11), the event list and the RecommendedBeta paragraph follow. The record still has 30 fields; RecommendedBeta differs from Strict in 13 of them (15 until the council's RB-S2 rulings of 2026-11-20 reverted `missing_iend` and `trns_invalid` to `Reject`).

Council rulings on the M1b questions (2026-11-20, `DECISIONS.md` "Council M1b-Q rulings"): `IgnoreTail` is narrowed to exactly-`R` output (`zlib_tail` row), and a bad-CRC fcTL in the frames constructor's walk is an APNG structural error `CrcMismatch` (`crc_ancillary` and `apng_errors` rows). Field values and the record are unchanged.

This draft extends PNG-CORE-SPEC draft 0.1 (`../spec-draft/SPEC.md`, P1–P21) the way the frozen DIB and TGA policy specs and the GIF policy draft extend their cores. The stable entry points `decode_still`/`decode_still16`/`decode_frames`/`icc_profile` stay Strict. Profiled entry points take one of four named profiles (`Strict`, `RecommendedBeta`, `GdkCompat-2.42.12`, `ImageCrateCompat-0.25.10`) and add `recovered: bool` to the still result and to every frame; the RGBA8 still, the RGBA16 still (`try_decode_still16_with_policy`, owner decision PNG-Q13), the frame iterator, the ICC call and `chunks` all take the profile. `chunks` yields every chunk whose framing is valid, in file order, each with a validation status (`Accepted`, or `Discarded` with its reason: bad CRC, misplaced, duplicate, invalid value), so glue cannot reinstate a chunk the profile discarded; chunks whose CRC did not verify are never `Accepted`. The oracle models neither the 16-bit policy entry point nor the status (no API-level model exists in `policy_oracle.py`). `Policy::default()` is Strict; the profile selector is `#[non_exhaustive]`; the 30-field record below is internal and constructible only under `#[cfg(test)]`. The reference model is `policy_oracle.py`; `validate.py` checks the Strict bridge, obligation R and agreement with the incumbents.

**Non-negotiable for every policy value**, including test-only records: P1's caps (`MAX_INPUT`, `MAX_DIMENSION`, `MAX_OUTPUT`, `MAX_FRAMES`, `MAX_TOTAL_PIXELS`, `MAX_ICC`) with their check order; P20's reservation sites and no other input-dependent allocation; every inflate is bounded by its output limit. In Strict and RecommendedBeta the limit is `L`: an attempt to emit a byte when `L` bytes are written is `ExcessImageData`, and reaching `L` does not end inflate successfully; decoding continues to the stream end and the Adler-32 check with no further output permitted. No profile raises the limit above `L` until a bounded surplus rule, its checksum behaviour and the R2 detector are specified; the compatibility options `zlib_tail = Ignore` and `stream_end = AtRaster` are the only candidates for that exception (D12, council decision 2026-11-17); no read beyond the slice, unchecked overflow or narrowing, panic or nontermination; work `steps ≤ c·(|b| + R + N + P)`. A native decoder's acceptance beyond these caps is not part of any profile (`vec:apng-bad/actl-too-many-frames` is accepted by the incumbents and excluded from agreement counts). Until 2026-11-17 `probe:dims-100000x1`, `-16385x16` and `vec:ihdr/width-16385` were in that list too; the per-side cap is now the wire limit 2^31 − 1 and the output cap 256 MiB (`DECISIONS.md`, Q5), so they fall inside the caps and count in agreement. libpng's own per-side limit of 1,000,000 is not a core cap (open: whether GdkCompat should carry it). The Python oracle allocates freely; its memory behaviour is not evidence about a Rust implementation.

## The `recovered` flag

For every retained record, the still result's `recovered` (and a frame's) is true **iff** at least one of these data-affecting events occurred while producing it:

- **R1 transparency dropped:** in a colour type 0, 2 or 3 image, a tRNS chunk was discarded or shortened under `crc_ancillary=Skip`, `ancillary_order`, `ancillary_duplicate=FirstWins` (a later tRNS) or `trns_invalid`.
- **R2 surplus image data discarded:** the image or frame's zlib stream produced more than `R` bytes and the surplus was discarded (`zlib_tail ∈ {Ignore, StopAtRaster}`, `stream_end=AtRaster`). Not reachable in RecommendedBeta: its `zlib_tail=IgnoreTail` keeps surplus decompressed output an error.
- **R3 palette substitution:** an index `≥ E` was drawn as `(0,0,0,255)` (`palette_index=Black`).
- **R4 animation fallback:** `apng_errors=Static` replaced an animation whose structure is invalid by the default image (frames entry only). RecommendedBeta reaches it (PNG-Q11).
- **R5 partial image:** rows that were never decoded were filled with `(0x97,0x98,0x99,0xFF)` (`image_data_error=GdkGrey`, `eof=GdkPartial`).

No other tolerance sets the flag: an ignored ancillary CRC on a chunk other than tRNS, dropped misplaced, duplicate or invalid metadata, trailing bytes, a missing or damaged IEND, a CINFO window violation, an ignored Adler-32 under `adler=Ignore`, APNG chunks ignored by `decode_still`, extra frames beyond `num_frames`. Strict never sets it. So `recovered=false` does not imply that Strict accepts the file.

## Residuals (measured, not reproduced)

- **GDK, the chunk that ends the IDAT run:** libpng's progressive reader reads that chunk's header itself (`pngpread.c:398–420`), without `png_check_chunk_name` or the length check, so a non-letter type there is not rejected (`held:075`: the image is complete and the file ends inside that chunk; GDK accepts, GdkCompat rejects with `InvalidChunkType`). If the zlib stream has not ended at that header, libpng stops with "Not enough compressed data" even when the file ends inside that chunk (`heldB:269`: GDK rejects, GdkCompat's `eof=GdkPartial` shows grey rows). Not modelled.
- **GDK, PLTE in a truecolour image:** libpng treats every PLTE error in colour types 0, 2, 4, 6 (duplicate, out of place, bad length) as benign and ignores the chunk (`pngrutil.c:1073–1084`); `plte_unexpected=Ignore` covers only PLTE in grey images and after IDAT (`held:279`, `heldB:274`: a duplicate PLTE in an RGB image). Not modelled.
- **image, a frame completed before a damaged chunk header:** image delivers an APNG frame whose zlib stream is complete even when the next chunk header is broken; `apng_errors=Prefix` closes a frame only at the next chunk (`held:047`: image shows 3 frames, ImageCrateCompat 2). Not modelled.
- **image, interlaced data that overruns the raster and is truncated:** png reports `InsufficientInput` when the stream produced more than `R` bytes and then ran out (`held:156`, a 1×1 interlaced image); `stream_end=AtRaster` accepts. Not modelled.
- **image panics** on a PLTE whose length is not a multiple of 3 (`vec:plte/length-8`, `probe:plte-len-13`, `-14`); the runner catches the panic, a real caller may not (DESIGN-NOTES N16). Every profile rejects these files.
- **GDK path dependence:** one-byte writes give each `inflate()` call one byte, so it returns for input before it can detect an error after a completed row; that row is then handed over (`probe:adler-bad`: the whole-buffer loader loses the last row, one-byte writes show the full image). One-byte writes also differ on `probe:zlib-window-too-small`. GdkCompat models the whole-buffer loader only; the forced-type loader passes the whole buffer in one call, while a loader that sniffs the type first passes the first `SNIFF_BUFFER_SIZE` bytes in a separate call (`gdk-pixbuf-loader.c:484–556`), which can move an `inflate()` boundary.
- **Inputs above 4 KiB** (three vectors, `probe:iccp-bomb-100m`, `ztxt-bomb-64m`, `itxt-bomb-64m`, `text-many-20000`, `text-large-8m` and six witnesses; 14 in all) are now compared: `results.json` stores them by reference and `validate.py` regenerates them (all four compatibility comparisons agree on 14/14). A rerun under a 3,000,000 KiB address-space limit (`probes/results-large.json`) gave the same outcome for every runner as the 768 MiB run. `probe:dims-100000x1` and `dims-16385x16` are at most 4 KiB and always had their bytes stored; they are excluded as beyond the P1 caps (every runner, glycin and `load_from_memory` included, accepts both).

## Not reproducible, explicitly excluded

- Acceptance beyond the P1 caps (image and GDK accept 16,385-pixel and 100,000-pixel widths, image accepts `num_frames` above 65,536); allocation failures (`PngDecoder` and glycin's loader code abort on `probe:dims-65535-square-tiny-data` under 768 MiB and 3,000,000 KiB address-space limits; `image::load_from_memory` returns "Memory limit exceeded"; GDK reports "Insufficient memory").
- libpng's warnings, gdk-pixbuf's option strings, image's metadata accessors: not part of the pixel result.
- libspng, stb, lodepng, zune and minipng behaviours (CRC indifference, inventing palettes, rejecting tRNS variants): measured in the earlier leniency run and here for libspng and stb; no profile targets them.

## Recorded decisions and open boundary

- D1: the stable entry points are Strict; profiled entry points serve named profiles only; arbitrary records are `#[cfg(test)]`.
- D2: RecommendedBeta contains only error-site relaxations; every pixel-rule field (`depth16`, `blend`, `dispose_previous`) keeps Strict's value. 16-bit to 8-bit is rounding in every profile except GdkCompat (`HighByte`, PNG-Q3); `blend=Exact` in every profile except ImageCrateCompat, and `ImageF32` enters ImageCrateCompat only if an exact integer characterisation of image's results is proved, otherwise it is dropped and recorded as a residual (PNG-Q8).
- D3: truncated image data, corrupt zlib data and a wrong Adler-32 remain errors in Strict and RecommendedBeta (PNG-Q10; `GdkCompat` may offer a grey fill later). APNG structure errors remain errors in Strict; RecommendedBeta replaces the animation by the default image with R4 when its walk finds one before any frame is exposed, and keeps every other animation failure an error (PNG-Q11).
- D4: `recovered` is the closed five-event list R1–R5.
- D5: profile names carry the measured library version and are immutable once frozen; `RecommendedBeta` is the owner's name for the provisional recommended profile.
- D6 (2026-11-16): compatibility profiles are deferred until Strict and RecommendedBeta are proved, then frozen opt-in after a held-out re-measurement and a per-field necessity check; their targets are libpng 1.6.48 through gdk-pixbuf 2.42.12's loader and png 0.18.1 through image 0.25.10. Retargeting to `gdk_pixbuf_new_from_file` is a different contract with a different name (PNG-Q14).
- Open: PNG-Q16 in `QUESTIONS.md`, and the items listed in `DECISIONS.md`.
