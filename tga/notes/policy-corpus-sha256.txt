# Policy corpus check with SHA-256 digest verification (2026-11-12)

Second item of the Sol soundness dispatch: `examples/policy_corpus.rs` checked
each file's byte length against manifest column 8 but not its SHA-256 (column 2).
The example now compares the digest of every file it reads against the manifest
and panics, naming the file, on a mismatch.

## What changed

- `tga_verus/examples/policy_corpus.rs` gains a std-only SHA-256
  (FIPS 180-4, no dependencies) and a `sha256_self_test` that runs first, over
  the two published short vectors (`""`, `"abc"`, the 56-byte two-block message)
  and the one-million-`a` vector. A wrong digest implementation fails the
  self-test before it can pass any corpus file.
- The per-row check is now `assert_eq!(sha256_hex(&bytes), columns[1], ...)`,
  in addition to the existing length check; the summary line prints
  `sha256=verified`.
- The corpus is read in place at `/home/ws/corpora/tga`; no corpus bytes are
  copied into the repo or `~/projects`.

## Verified

(`src/`) and not an obligation. The library and the Verus proof are unchanged;
this does not consume a verifier run and the run-194 green state stands.
